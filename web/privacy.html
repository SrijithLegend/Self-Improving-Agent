Privacy policy

This page explains what this website and the hosted SIA service collect, why, where it is kept and how you can see or delete it. If something here is unclear, ask us.

Who we are

SIA is built and run by Srijith, in India ("we", "us"). We decide how the data described here is used.

If you run SIA yourself (self-hosting), you are the operator of your copy. Your copy never sends data to us, and this policy does not cover it.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts.
  • Your light or dark theme and the Human or Agent view are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never reach us.
  • The fonts load from Google Fonts. To send them, Google receives your IP address and browser details, under Google's privacy policy (opens in a new tab).
  • The "Request access" form sends what you type to our server. The next section explains what happens to it.

The Request access form

  • What we collect: your email address and name, and if you give them, your company and what you'd use SIA for. A hidden field that only bots fill in is checked and then discarded. Your IP address is counted in memory for up to an hour to limit abuse, and is not stored with your request.
  • Why: to decide on your request, reply to you, and set up your organization if we approve it. We don't use it for marketing and don't share it with anyone.
  • How long: a pending request is kept until we decide. An approved request is kept with your account until you close it. A rejected request is deleted 90 days after the decision.
  • Deleting it: write to us from the same email address and we delete your request within 30 days.

Before your app's data is stored, SIA removes secrets, card numbers, email addresses and phone numbers by default, plus any patterns you add. This runs in the SDK and again on the server.

Model provider keys

Calls that use a language model take your provider key in the X-LLM-Key header. We use it for that one request and never store or log it. The text of that request goes to the provider you chose (for example Google, Anthropic or OpenAI), under your agreement with them.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay (opens in a new tab). Your card, UPI or bank details go to Razorpay, never to our server.

Where it is stored and who else sees it

Hosted data lives in one database on the server that runs the service. We will name the hosting provider and region on this page before the hosted service opens.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. The only other parties that process it are the hosting provider, Razorpay for payments and the model provider you choose. We share data with authorities only when Indian law requires it.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete agents, documents, examples and dataset examples yourself, in the API or the dashboard.
  • To close your account and delete everything, email us from the address on your account. We delete it within 30 days. Copies in backups are gone within a further 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent you gave. These rights come from India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, if you are in the European Union, the GDPR. Write to us. We answer within 30 days. Please don't post personal data in a public GitHub issue.

Grievance contact: questions or complaints about how we handle your data go to the issue tracker on GitHub. We answer within 30 days.

Children

SIA is for businesses and developers. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you already gave us, we email account holders before it takes effect.
