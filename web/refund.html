Refunds and cancellation

How refunds work for the hosted SIA service. The same rules are in section 7 of the terms of service. Self-hosting SIA is free, so none of this applies to it.

The policy

  • Nothing to cancel. Paid plans are 30-day passes. They don't renew automatically and we never charge you again on our own, so there is no subscription to cancel. When a pass ends, your account returns to the Free plan's limits.
  • Unused passes. You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of paying if you haven't used the paid limits.
  • Downtime. If the hosted service is unavailable for more than 24 hours in total during a paid period because of a fault on our side, we refund the time it was unavailable, pro rata, on request. For example, 3 days down in a 30-day pass refunds 3/30 of its price.
  • Everything else. Other payments aren't refundable, except where the law requires.
  • How refunds are paid. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5 to 7 working days.

How to ask for a refund

Write to us from the email address on your account. Include your organization name and the payment reference from Plans & billing in the dashboard (it starts with order_ or pay_). We reply within 7 days. Please don't post payment details in a public GitHub issue.
